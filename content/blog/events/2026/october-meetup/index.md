## 🤖 Community Assembly: Running Local LLMs

*Presented by Alex*

Local LLMs are much more accessible than you would think. They give you a way to take control of your privacy, and they let you AI-assist tasks that the cloud providers will outright refuse. Depending on what you want to do, getting started may be as simple as installing a program on your laptop.

Alex walks through the whole path, from the concepts to a working setup:

- **What an LLM is** — the high-level concept, without the hype
- **What a `.gguf`, `.safetensor`, or `.mmproj` file is** — and where to get them
- **Running LM Studio and Ollama** — the two easiest on-ramps
- **"My hardware is X — what models can I run?"** — matching models to the machine you already own
- **Tool calls, MCP servers, and RAG** — Model Context Protocol and Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and what they unlock
- **Uncensored models** — why they are a genuinely useful tool, not just a novelty
- **OpenRouter and harnesses** — how to use OpenRouter, plus a look at Pi, Off Grid AI, LocalMind, and friends

Bring your laptop and your hardware questions. This one is built for following along.
