# 🏠 August Recap: Home Assistant, a New Secretary, and Node-Host Outreach

**📅 Wednesday, August 5, 2026**
**📍 Kent/Thomas Room @ Unitarian Universalist Church, Concord**

Seven of us gathered for August's meetup — a smaller group, but a productive one. The evening covered a home automation deep-dive, some organizational housekeeping, and the launch of a new working group focused on growing the mesh.

---

## 🏠 Community Assembly: Home Assistant

*Presented by Alex*

Alex walked the group through **Home Assistant**, the open-source home automation platform, sharing how he uses it day to day and demoing some of its features. It was a practical, example-driven session — less theory, more "here's what this actually does for me."

One detail got people's attention: someone in the broader community has already built a **MeshCore integration** for Home Assistant. For a group that spends most of its time thinking about mesh networking, seeing it show up as a plug-in for home automation was a fun surprise — and a reminder of how much is already being built around these tools.

---

## 🤝 Members' Council

The Council covered a good mix of organizational and technical updates:

- **Becky volunteered to serve as Secretary** and proposed that CRM start tracking expenses and donations formally. The group agreed to run this as a **3-month trial**.
- The group talked through hosting documents — including meeting minutes — somewhere other than Google. **Alex volunteered to look into a Box subscription** for a CRM knowledge base.
- There was discussion of ways to make it easier to accept donations, including whether something like Buy Me a Coffee would be a good fit.
- The **Reticulum** and **LoRa** working groups each gave informational updates on where things stand.
- A **new working group** is spinning up to draft a form letter for reaching out to potential node hosts — folks willing to host mesh infrastructure on their property. **Adam is coordinating**, joined by Jason, Tyler, and Ken. They're aiming to bring a draft letter to the Members' Council in September.
- **Adam is now hosting a weekly voice net**, Thursdays at 7pm, in the CRM room on his Mumble server at `home.tobias.im`.

---

## 🛠️ Working Session

Rather than a separate working session, the evening's momentum went straight into standing up the new node-host outreach working group — Adam, Jason, Tyler, and Ken got organized around next steps for the form letter.

---
