## 🧭 Community Assembly: Systems Thinking for AI Success

*Presented by Sam*

Most people interact with LLMs one prompt at a time — a chatbot pattern of back-and-forth questions and answers. Sam's talk makes the case for a different mental model: treating your work with LLMs and agents as a **system**, with feedback loops, constraints, and structure, rather than a single conversation to get right in one shot.

Expect a practical look at what systems thinking brings to working with AI agents in particular, and why it tends to produce better, more reliable outcomes than the default chatbot pattern most people fall into.
